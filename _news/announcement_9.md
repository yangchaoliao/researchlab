Our collaborative paper "[Clasping folds enable superior elastic strength of graphene films](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11109-y)" was published in [Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11109-y)! Dr. Yangchao is a co-first author of this work. The study introduces a "clasping folds" architecture that enables graphene films to achieve high strength (2.9 GPa), high toughness (84.5 MJ/m^3), and high elasticity (5%) simultaneously. Molecular dynamics simulations revealed a unique "pull-to-push" load transfer mechanism, leading to a new "shear-lock" model with 96% stress transfer efficiency. This work breaks the classic strength–toughness trade-off in layered 2D materials and opens a new design paradigm from "planar stacking" to "spatial configuration engineering," with potential applications in lightweight aerospace structures, high-performance composites, and impact-resistant protection.
